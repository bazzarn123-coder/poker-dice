Poker Dice Rules

Take turns rolling 5 poker dice. You have up to 3 rolls per turn to make your best 5-card poker hand.

Tap dice to HOLD them between rolls. Locked dice stay on the table with a cyan highlight ring.

Hand Ranks (Highest to Lowest):
1. 5 of a Kind A - A - A - A - A
2. 4 of a Kind K - K - K - K - 9
3. Full House Q - Q - Q - J - J
4. Straight A-K-Q-J-10 or K-Q-J-10-9
5. 3 of a Kind J - J - J - A - 10
6. Two Pair 10 - 10 - 9 - 9 - K
7. One Pair 9 - 9 - A - K - Q
8. High Card Highest face value

PLAYER 1 WINS!
